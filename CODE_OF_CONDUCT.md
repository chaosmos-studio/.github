# Code of conduct

Chaosmos makes games for everyone, and the places around them should feel the same.
This code applies in our repositories, in r/chaosmos_studios, under our social media posts, and anywhere else you speak with or for the studio.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient, most of all with people who are new or stuck.
- Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- Make feedback useful: what happened, what you expected, and what would be better.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs and personal attacks.
- Sexual remarks, images or attention nobody asked for.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, scams, cheats and pirated copies of games.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find threatening or degrading.

## Reporting

Write to [hello@chaosmos.studio](mailto:hello@chaosmos.studio).
Say what happened and where, with links or screenshots if you have them.
Shravan Goswami reads every report and keeps it private.

## What happens next

Depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before, we may remove the content, warn the person, or ban them from our spaces for a while or for good.

This code draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
